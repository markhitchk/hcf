Privacy Policy

How Harley's Clan Forum collects, uses, protects, and manages information associated with forum accounts and services.

Effective  January 1, 2025forum.harleytg.com
Privacy notice: We are committed to protecting user information and explaining how account, usage, security, and support data may be handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Harley's Clan Forum, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When logging in using Discord, Google, or our system, we may collect details such as username, user ID, avatar, email address, and information provided during signup or login.
  • Personal Information: If additional information is submitted while using our services, such as feedback or support requests, we may collect that data.
  • Usage Data: Information about interactions with our services, including logins, theme preferences, and feature usage.
  • IP Addresses and Device History: IP addresses and device history may be collected for security and monitoring purposes. You may request deletion through a support request where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticating and providing account access.
  • Improving and personalizing the user experience.
  • Notifying users about updates and service changes.
  • Monitoring usage and preventing technical or security issues.
  • Responding to support requests and feedback.

3. Signup and Login Options

DiscordQuick access using a connected Discord account.
Password-BasedTraditional account registration using a password.
PasswordlessLogin using a secure, time-sensitive email link.

Depending on the chosen method, authentication-related account identifiers may be stored to maintain account access and security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and data portability.

7. Security of Your Information

We implement reasonable security measures intended to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page and users are encouraged to review it regularly.
