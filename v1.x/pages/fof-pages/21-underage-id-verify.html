Parental Consent & ID Verification

Effective Date: 1/1/2024

Welcome to Harley's Clan Forum. In compliance with U.S. law, all users under the age of 18 must obtain verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian before accessing our services.

1. Who Needs Parental Consent?

Any user under the age of 18 must have explicit parental consent before using the platform.

  • Users under 13: COPPA requires parental consent before collecting personal data.
  • Users aged 13-17: While COPPA doesn't apply, we still require parental consent for protection.

2. How Parental Consent Works

  1. Child Registration: The underage user enters a parent or guardian’s email.
  2. Support Team Verification: Our support team may request identity verification.
  3. Consent Confirmation: Parent confirms consent via email link.
  4. Account Activation: Once verified, the account is activated with appropriate restrictions.

3. Underage Detection and Consequences

  • Account Suspension or Ban: Accounts may be suspended until consent is verified.
  • Required Verification: If detected in gameplay, Discord VC, or forum activity, parental consent must be confirmed.

4. ID Verification for Users Over 18

Harley’s Clan may request ID verification for users over 18 to ensure accurate age representation and maintain a secure community environment.

  • To confirm a user's age if suspected to be underage.
  • Case-by-case verification for community safety.

ID verification is handled through Discord support tickets in the official Harley's Clan Discord server.

5. Privacy & Data Protection

Information collected during parental verification or ID verification is securely handled and used only for consent confirmation or age verification. It will not be shared with third parties.

6. Legal References

  • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • Minor Consent Regulations (13-17)
  • U.S. Online Safety & Privacy Regulations

7. Parent / Guardian Instructions

  1. Check your email for the verification message.
  2. Submit proof of guardianship if requested.
  3. Click the confirmation link to approve consent.
  4. Once verified, the child’s account will be activated.

Appeal Process

  1. Follow instructions in the system email regarding the suspension.
  2. Submit an appeal at banned.harleytg.com
  3. Appeal through the official Harley's Clan Discord Server

8. Addressing Past Security Issues

In 2023, Harley's Clan experienced an incident involving individuals who attempted to manipulate the system by posing as older users. Following this event, the community migrated servers and implemented stronger verification procedures.

9. Communication Policies

Direct messaging from underage users is not allowed. All communication must occur in monitored public channels on the forum or official Discord server.

Content involving minors remains PG-13. Adult discussions may occur only in restricted channels when minors are not present.

10. Transparency & Reporting

Community members are encouraged to report suspicious behavior. This transparency system has helped maintain a safe environment and prevent exploitation.
