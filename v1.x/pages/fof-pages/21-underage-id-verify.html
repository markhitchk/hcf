Verification Policy

Parental Consent & ID Verification

Information about age-related consent, verification, account restrictions, privacy, and support procedures used by Harley's Clan Forum.

Effective  January 1, 2024Safety Policy
Important: Verification requests should be handled only through official Harley's Clan Forum support channels. Do not post identity documents or private personal information publicly.

1. Who Needs Parental Consent?

Users who do not meet the forum's age and consent requirements may be required to obtain approval from a parent or legal guardian before using certain services.

Users Under 13Parental consent requirements may apply before personal information is collected.
Users Ages 13–17Additional parental awareness or consent may be required under forum policy.

2. How Parental Consent Works

  1. Registration: The account may be asked to provide a parent or guardian contact method.
  2. Support Verification: The support team may request additional information where needed.
  3. Consent Confirmation: A parent or guardian confirms the required consent.
  4. Account Access: Access may be restored or activated once the required verification is complete.

3. Underage Detection & Account Restrictions

  • Accounts may be temporarily suspended or restricted while required consent is being verified.
  • Verification may be requested after information indicating that an account holder does not meet age requirements.

4. ID or Age Verification

Harley's Clan Forum may request age or identity verification on a case-by-case basis for community safety, account ownership, or age-related access decisions.

Private information: Never submit identity documents in a public discussion, profile, or public Discord channel.

5. Privacy & Data Protection

Information submitted for consent or age verification should be used only for the verification or safety purpose for which it was requested and handled through authorized support channels.

6. Parent / Guardian Instructions

  1. Review the verification or consent request.
  2. Follow the instructions sent through the official support or verification process.
  3. Provide only the information specifically requested.
  4. Wait for confirmation that the account has been reviewed.

8. Communication & Safety

Community communication involving younger users must follow forum rules and safety requirements. Report suspicious behavior or requests for private information to staff.
