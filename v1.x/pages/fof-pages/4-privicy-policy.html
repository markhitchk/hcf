Privacy Policy for Harley's Clan Forum

Effective Date: 1/1/2025

At Harley's Clan Forum, accessible from https://forum.harleytg.online/ , we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share your information, including information collected through Discord, Google, and our own system logins/signups.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Harley's Clan Forum, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When logging in using Discord, Google, or our system we collect details such as username, user ID, avatar, email address, and information provided during signup or login.
  • Personal Information: If additional information is submitted while using our services (such as feedback or support requests) we may collect that data.
  • Usage Data: Information about your interactions with our services including logins, theme preferences, and feature usage.
  • IP Addresses and Device History: We collect IP addresses and device history for security and monitoring purposes. IP records may be terminated at any time. You may request deletion through a support request.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for purposes including:

  • Authenticating and providing account access
  • Improving and personalizing user experience
  • Notifying users about updates and service changes
  • Monitoring usage and preventing technical issues
  • Responding to support requests and feedback

3. Signup and Login Options

We offer several login methods:

  • Discord: Quick access using Discord credentials.
  • Password-Based: Traditional account registration with password.
  • Passwordless: Login using a secure time-sensitive email link.

Depending on the chosen method we may store authentication data such as Discord ID, Google ID, email address, or password to maintain account access and security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including legal and regulatory requirements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights regarding your personal data including:

  • Access to your personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of personal data
  • Restriction of data processing
  • Data portability

7. Security of Your Information

We implement reasonable security measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page and users are encouraged to review it regularly.
