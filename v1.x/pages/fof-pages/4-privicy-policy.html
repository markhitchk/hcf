Privacy Policy for Harley's Clan Forum

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

At Harley's Clan Forum, accessible from https://forum.harleytg.com/, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share information, including information collected through third-party sign-in providers and our own account system.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Harley's Clan Forum, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Details such as username, user ID, avatar, email address, and information provided during signup or login.
  • Personal Information: Information submitted through feedback, support requests, forms, or other forum features.
  • Usage Data: Information about interactions with the forum, including logins, preferences, and feature usage.
  • IP Addresses and Device History: Security-related network and device information may be recorded to protect accounts and the service. Where supported, deletion requests may be submitted through support.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticating and providing account access
  • Improving and personalizing the user experience
  • Notifying users about updates and service changes
  • Monitoring usage, security, abuse, and technical issues
  • Responding to support requests and feedback

3. Signup and Login Options

  • Discord: Sign in using a connected Discord account when available.
  • Password-Based: Traditional account registration using a password.
  • Passwordless: Login using a secure time-sensitive email link when available.

Depending on the method used, we may store identifiers such as a connected provider ID, email address, and account authentication data needed to maintain access and security. Passwords are handled by the forum authentication system rather than displayed as readable text.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or authorities when necessary to operate the forum, protect users, comply with law, or enforce policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for forum operation, security, account management, dispute handling, and applicable legal requirements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability. Some records may need to be retained for security, abuse prevention, or legal reasons.

7. Security of Your Information

We use reasonable safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse of personal data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with revised policy information when appropriate.
