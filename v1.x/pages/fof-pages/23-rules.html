HARLEY-THE-GAMER'S RULES

Community Directives

LEADERSHIP: HARLEY-THE-GAMER aka HarleyTG

1. Cheating and Hacking

1.1 Prohibited Activities: Cheating, hacking, exploiting, or intentionally gaining an unfair advantage is prohibited.

1.2 Ban Inquiries: If you have been banned, contact support for a formal inquiry or appeal.

2. Conduct & Staff

2.1 No Backseat Moderation: Allow admins and moderators to handle moderation issues.

2.2 Inventory Checks: Where applicable to game/server activities, staff may request to review relevant inventory or account evidence when investigating a rule violation.

STRIKE POLICY: Two warnings may result in a one-week restriction depending on the violation and moderation context.

3. Respect & Integrity

3.1 Standards: Bullying, targeted harassment, discrimination, or repeated abusive behavior is not allowed. Keep public names and content appropriate for the community.

3.2 Streaming: No stream sniping or intentional disruption of another user's stream. Follow the applicable platform rules.

4. Communication

Chat / Voice: Abusive language, threats, spam, or repeated disruption may result in a mute, restriction, or ban. Do not post unauthorized advertisements, suspicious links, or unrelated server invites.

5. Commercial Protocol

Scamming, deceptive trading, and unsolicited direct-message advertising are prohibited and may trigger immediate moderation review.

Directive Context

By participating in Harley's Clan Forum community spaces, you agree to follow these rules and related forum policies. Rules may be updated as the community, forum features, and connected services change.
