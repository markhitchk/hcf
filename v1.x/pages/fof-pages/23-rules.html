Official Community Rules

Core expectations for fair play, respectful conduct, communication, moderation, and community safety.

Effective  October 31, 2024Leadership: HarleyTG

1. Cheating & Hacking

1.1 Prohibited Activities: Cheating, hacking, or exploiting an unfair advantage is prohibited.

1.2 Ban Inquiries: If you have been banned, use the official support or appeal process for review.

2. Conduct & Staff

2.1 No Backseat Moderation: Allow moderators and admins to handle enforcement issues.

2.2 Inventory Checks: Where applicable to a supported game/server, staff may request an inventory review as part of moderation.

Strike policy: Two warnings may result in a one-week restriction depending on the rule and circumstances.

3. Respect & Integrity

  • Community standards: Bullying and harassment are not allowed. Keep public names, builds, and community-facing content appropriate.
  • Streaming: Do not stream snipe. Respect the terms and rules of the platform or game being used.

4. Communication

Abusive language may result in moderation action. Do not distribute unauthorized spam, external server invitations, or harmful links.

5. Commercial Conduct

Scamming and unsolicited direct-message advertising are prohibited and may result in immediate review or moderation action.

Directive context: By participating in the community, you agree to follow these rules so the forum and connected community spaces remain safe and usable. Rules may be updated as the community changes.
