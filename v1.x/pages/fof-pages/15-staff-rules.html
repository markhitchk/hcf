Moderator & Admin Rules

Standards for fair moderation, staff conduct, privacy, escalation, and community leadership.

20 Staff RulesModerators & Admins

General Conduct

  1. Be Professional & Respectful: Treat all users with respect. No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of power.
  2. Lead by Example: Maintain a positive, constructive environment and follow the same rules as members.
  3. No Favoritism: Apply rules fairly and do not give special treatment to friends or selected users.
  4. Stay Active & Engaged: Participate, assist users, and help maintain a safe environment.

Moderation Duties

  1. Enforce Forum Rules: Take appropriate action against violations, including warnings, mutes, or bans when justified.
  2. Keep Forums Organized: Move, merge, or lock threads when necessary.
  3. Monitor Reports & Complaints: Review and respond to user reports in a timely manner.
  4. No Unjustified Actions: Provide valid reasons and document warnings, mutes, bans, or other significant actions.
  5. Follow the Escalation Process: Escalate issues that are beyond your authority.

Admin-Specific Rules

  1. Maintain Technical Integrity: Do not make unauthorized changes to settings, permissions, or user accounts.
  2. Fair & Transparent Decisions: Major rules, bans, and policy decisions should be discussed appropriately within the admin team.
  3. Protect User Privacy: Never leak private messages, IP addresses, or personal data.
  4. No Abuse of Power: Do not edit or delete posts unfairly or ban users without a valid reason.
  5. Support & Guide Moderators: Help moderators follow staff guidelines correctly.

Conflict Resolution

  1. Do Not Engage in Arguments: Handle disagreements privately and professionally.
  2. Use Internal Discussions: Bring major disputes or concerns to other staff before acting where appropriate.
  3. Appeals & Unbans: Review appeals fairly and consider the available evidence.

Miscellaneous

  1. No Unauthorized Self-Promotion: Do not use staff status to promote personal content or services.
  2. Stay Updated: Keep up with staff meetings, announcements, and policy changes.
  3. If You Resign or Are Removed: Respect the decision and do not disrupt the forum or staff team.
Staff standard: Moderation authority exists to protect the community and enforce rules consistently—not to provide personal advantage.
