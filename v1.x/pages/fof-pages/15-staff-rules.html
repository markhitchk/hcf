=====================================
Moderator & Admin Rules - Harley's Forum
=====================================

== General Conduct ==

  1. Be Professional & Respectful – Treat all users with respect. No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of power.
  2. Lead by Example – Maintain a positive and constructive environment. Follow the same rules as members.
  3. No Favoritism – Do not give special treatment to friends or certain users. Apply rules fairly to everyone.
  4. Stay Active & Engaged – Regularly participate in discussions, assist users, and ensure a safe environment.

== Moderation Duties ==

  1. Enforce Forum Rules – Take appropriate action against rule violations, such as issuing warnings, mutes, or bans.
  2. Keep Forums Organized – Move, merge, or lock threads when necessary to keep discussions relevant and structured.
  3. Monitor Reports & Complaints – Review and respond to user reports in a timely manner.
  4. No Unjustified Actions – Provide valid reasons when issuing warnings, mutes, or bans. Always document actions.
  5. Follow the Escalation Process – If an issue is beyond your authority, escalate it to a higher-ranking admin.

== Admin-Specific Rules ==

  1. Maintain Technical Integrity – Do not make unauthorized changes to the forum’s settings, permissions, or user accounts.
  2. Fair & Transparent Decision-Making – Ensure rule changes, bans, and major decisions are discussed within the admin team.
  3. Protect User Privacy – Never leak private messages, IP addresses, or any personal data.
  4. No Abuse of Power – Do not edit or delete posts unfairly, and do not ban users without a valid reason.
  5. Support & Guide Moderators – Assist moderators when needed and ensure they are following guidelines properly.

== Conflict Resolution ==

  1. Do Not Engage in Arguments – If a disagreement arises, handle it privately and professionally.
  2. Use Internal Discussions – Bring up major disputes or concerns with other staff members before taking action.
  3. Appeals & Unbans – Users should have the right to appeal bans; handle appeals fairly and review all evidence.

== Miscellaneous ==

  1. No Unauthorized Self-Promotion – Do not use your position to promote personal content or services.
  2. Stay Updated – Keep up with staff meetings, announcements, and any policy changes.
  3. If You Resign or Are Removed – Respect the decision and do not cause drama or disrupt the forum.
