Staff Operations Directive

Moderator & Admin Rules

These rules apply to moderators and administrators of Harley's Clan Forum. Staff are expected to enforce community rules fairly, protect user privacy, document moderation actions, and avoid misuse of elevated permissions.

General Conduct

  1. Be Professional & Respectful – Treat all users with respect. No harassment, discrimination, or abuse of power.
  2. Lead by Example – Maintain a positive and constructive environment. Follow the same rules as members.
  3. No Favoritism – Do not give special treatment to friends or certain users. Apply rules fairly to everyone.
  4. Stay Active & Engaged – Regularly participate in discussions, assist users, and help maintain a safe environment.

Moderation Duties

  1. Enforce Forum Rules – Take appropriate action against rule violations, including warnings, mutes, restrictions, or bans when justified.
  2. Keep Forums Organized – Move, merge, or lock threads when necessary to keep discussions relevant and structured.
  3. Monitor Reports & Complaints – Review and respond to user reports in a timely manner.
  4. No Unjustified Actions – Provide valid reasons for warnings, mutes, bans, or other moderation actions and document them appropriately.
  5. Follow the Escalation Process – If an issue is beyond your authority, escalate it to a higher-ranking administrator.

Admin-Specific Rules

  1. Maintain Technical Integrity – Do not make unauthorized changes to forum settings, permissions, or user accounts.
  2. Fair & Transparent Decision-Making – Major rule changes, bans, and high-impact decisions should be discussed within the admin team when practical.
  3. Protect User Privacy – Never leak private messages, IP addresses, account information, or other personal data.
  4. No Abuse of Power – Do not edit or delete posts unfairly or ban users without a valid moderation reason.
  5. Support & Guide Moderators – Assist moderators when needed and make sure staff guidelines are being followed.

Conflict Resolution

  1. Do Not Engage in Arguments – Handle staff disagreements privately and professionally.
  2. Use Internal Discussions – Bring major disputes or concerns to other staff members before taking irreversible action when possible.
  3. Appeals & Unbans – Users should be able to appeal bans or major moderation actions; review appeals fairly and consider the available evidence.

Miscellaneous

  1. No Unauthorized Self-Promotion – Do not use a staff role to improperly promote personal content or services.
  2. Stay Updated – Keep up with staff announcements, policy changes, and internal guidance.
  3. If You Resign or Are Removed – Respect the decision and do not disrupt the forum, misuse retained access, or create unnecessary conflict.
STAFF NOTE: Elevated permissions exist to protect and operate the community, not to provide personal advantage.
