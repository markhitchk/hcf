Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by your browser when you visit a website. They can help maintain login sessions, remember preferences, support security features, and enable site functionality.

2. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential & Session Cookies

  • Forum session cookies: Used to keep your signed-in session working and protect account actions.
  • Remember-me cookies: Used when you choose to remain signed in between browser sessions.

Preference Cookies

  • Theme, appearance, language, or other interface preferences may be stored in cookies or local browser storage depending on the enabled forum extensions.

Security & Third-Party Cookies

  • Security, anti-abuse, authentication, embedded content, or connected services may set their own cookies when those features are used.

Cookie names and expiration periods can change as Flarum, extensions, hosting, and connected services are updated.

3. How We Use Cookies

  • Keep you logged in securely.
  • Remember supported forum preferences.
  • Protect accounts and reduce abuse or fraudulent activity.
  • Enable forum features and connected services.
  • Improve reliability and performance where applicable.

4. Managing Cookies

You can review, block, or delete cookies using your browser's privacy settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent sign-in or cause some forum features to stop working correctly.

For browser-specific instructions, open the privacy or site-data section of your browser settings or visit the browser vendor's official support documentation.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when forum software, extensions, hosting, or connected services change. The Last Updated date above will be revised when this page receives a material update.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, visit Harley's Clan Forum Support.
