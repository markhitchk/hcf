Cookie Policy

Last Updated: [2/2/2025]

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognize your preferences, maintain security, and improve site functionality.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

  • flarum_session – Maintains your session and login state. (Expires: Short-term session-based)
  • flarum_remember – Keeps you logged in after closing the browser. (Expires: Long-term)

Preference Cookies

  • flarum_nightmode – Saves your night mode preference. (Expires: End of session)

Security & Performance Cookies

  • isg – Used for session integrity and security. (Expires: August 17, 2025)
  • tfstk – Used for session tracking and security validation. (Expires: August 17, 2025)

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in securely.
  • Save your night mode preferences.
  • Improve security and prevent fraud.
  • Analyze website traffic and enhance performance.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to log in or use certain features.

  • Manage cookies in Chrome
  • Manage cookies in Firefox
  • Manage cookies in Edge
  • Manage cookies in Safari
