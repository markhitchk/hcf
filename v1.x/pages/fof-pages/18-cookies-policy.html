Cookie Policy

How browser cookies and similar storage may be used to maintain sessions, preferences, security, and forum functionality.

Last Updated  August 11, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of browser data used by websites to remember session information, preferences, and other information needed for site functionality.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

EssentialUsed to maintain session and authentication state. Flarum installations may use session or remember-me cookies for this purpose.
PreferencesUsed by the forum or enabled extensions to remember interface choices such as display preferences.
Security & PerformanceAdditional services may use browser storage for security validation, abuse prevention, or reliable service operation.

3. How We Use Cookies

  • Keep signed-in sessions working securely.
  • Remember supported forum preferences.
  • Help protect accounts and services from abuse.
  • Maintain and improve site functionality and reliability.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent login or cause parts of the forum to stop working correctly.

Browser setting: Cookie controls are managed by your browser or device. The exact menu differs between Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, and other browsers.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when forum software, extensions, or supporting services change.
