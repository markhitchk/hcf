📌 Types of Requests & What to Include

Support Requests

General Inquiries
For questions about community guidelines, partnerships, or other non-account topics, provide a short and clear explanation of what you need.

Feedback & Suggestions
For improvement ideas, explain the feature or change you are suggesting and how it would help the forum or community.
